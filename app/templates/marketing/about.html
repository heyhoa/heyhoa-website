About

We started {% include 'partials/hey-mark.html' %} because the treasurer quit.

A 48-unit townhome community in Texas. Self-managed for twelve years. The treasurer moved away and nobody would take the job because the job was a second job.

The quotes from management companies were four figures a month for a person who would visit once and a back office nobody would ever meet. The quotes from remote accounting firms were private, took a week, and still did not answer the phone when an owner called.

So we built the back office we wanted: public pricing, financials on a date you can plan around, and an assistant that has actually read the CC&Rs. {% include 'partials/hey-mark.html' %} does the reading, the coding and the first draft of everything. People do the judgment and the signatures.

We are a small team of HOA accountants, community managers who got tired of the model, and engineers. We work remotely across the US. We are not a law firm, a CPA firm or a property manager, and we are careful to say so.

What we believe

  • Boards should see every number and the document behind it
  • Pricing you have to ask for is pricing that depends on who is asking
  • A promise is only a promise if missing it costs us something
  • Owners deserve an answer in minutes, not a ticket number
  • Automation should make the audit trail better, not thinner

How it works

Three weeks to switch. Then the 5th, every month.

You keep paying bills the old way until we say switch. Nothing breaks in the middle.

  1. Quote (day 0)

    Three-minute form. Door count, what you do today, what hurts. You get a written proposal the same day with the exact monthly number.

  2. Documents and access (week 1)

    Governing docs, last 12 months of bank statements, vendor list, owner roster, the QuickBooks file if there is one. Read-only bank access is set up with your bank, not through us.

  3. Parallel rebuild (week 2)

    {% include 'partials/hey-mark.html' %} rebuilds the books from the bank statements and invoices while your old process keeps running. Duplicates and miscodes show up here; boards usually find money.

  4. Reconcile and go live (week 3)

    We reconcile to your last statement to the penny, load owners into the portal, and send the first welcome email. Autopay enrollment opens the same day.

  5. Monthly rhythm

    Invoices are coded the day they arrive. The board approves payments in the portal. Owner questions are answered as they come in. The packet lands by the 5th. Board meets with current numbers.

Who you talk to

A person, plus {% include 'partials/hey-mark.html' %}

Your board has a named coordinator. Owners get {% include 'partials/hey-mark.html' %} first and a person second. Nothing lands in a queue.

Where the money sits

Your bank, your name

We never hold funds. Operating and reserve accounts stay in the association's name at your bank, with the board as signers.

If you leave

Thirty days, full export

GL, owner ledgers, documents and vendor files in standard formats. We help the next firm load it.

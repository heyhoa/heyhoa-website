These terms sit under the Professional Services Agreement each association signs (read it). Where they differ, the signed agreement controls.

1. What hey hoa is

{{ brand.name }} provides accounting, administrative and software services to community associations, in an administrative and advisory capacity. We are software plus a service: software that keeps the books, portals and agents; people who review, reconcile and execute. We do not provide legal, tax, insurance-adjusting, engineering or property-management services, and nothing in the portals is legal or tax advice. The board governs; we keep the books and do what the board decides.

2. Money

Association funds are held in accounts in the association's legal name and tax ID at an FDIC-insured bank. Disbursements are made only for board-approved bills, insurance, taxes, statutory items and our fee, after the association's approval rules are met. Transfers between accounts and purchases or redemptions of CDs and other instruments are executed by a person at the bank after a recorded board decision. Our software never initiates a transfer, wire or payment on its own. Opening deposits and the final transfer of funds from a prior bank are made by check, by the association's signers.

3. Accounts, access and actions

The association decides who may log in, approve, vote and sign, and keeps that list current. Each user keeps their credentials secret, uses the multi-factor authentication we offer, and tells us at once if a device or mailbox is lost or compromised. Actions taken through an association user's account are the association's actions. If a third party obtains a user's credentials or control of a user's email and uses them to approve a payment, change a vendor's bank details, send a message or alter a setting, that act is attributed to the association, and {{ brand.name }} is not liable for the resulting loss. Approval rules, dual approvals and positive pay are controls the association chooses in its settings; they reduce risk, they do not transfer it.

Buy cyber insurance. We recommend every association carry a cyber policy that covers social engineering and funds-transfer fraud, in addition to fidelity coverage. The insurance page in the board portal links to a quote, and stores the policy once bound. Carrying it is the association's decision and responsibility.

4. Automation and AI

Software, including machine learning models, reads documents, codes transactions, drafts communications and proposes actions. Anything that moves money, changes an owner's balance, sends a notice with a fee, or binds the association waits for a person's review or the board's approval under its rules. Every automated action is logged and reviewable by the board. Model providers do not train on your data.

5. Your data

The association's data (ledgers, owner records, documents, messages) belongs to the association and exports in standard formats on request, including after termination. We use it only to deliver the service, to meet legal and audit duties, and to improve the service in aggregate. We do not sell it. Processors we rely on (bank, payment processor, mail house, accounting platform, model providers) work under contracts that limit their use of it. Owners' personal data is handled per the privacy policy.

6. Fees

Tier prices and owner-charged fees are published at /pricing and change only with sixty days' written notice. Physical mail, certified mail, bank fees and similar third-party costs are passed through at cost. If the monthly financial package is delivered after the 5th business day for a month in which the association gave us bank access and documents on time, that month's base fee is waived.

7. Term and ending

Twelve months, then month to month. Either party may end the agreement with thirty days' written notice; alleged breaches get sixty days to cure. On ending we deliver the books, documents and owner records in standard formats within thirty days and cooperate with the successor.

8. Limits

Our total liability for any claim arising from the service is limited to the fees the association paid us in the twelve months before the claim. We are not liable for indirect, consequential or punitive damages, for losses caused by the association's or its users' acts or omissions (including compromised credentials and instructions we reasonably believed came from an authorized user), for third-party services, or for events beyond our reasonable control. The association indemnifies us for claims arising from its instructions, its users' actions and its governing documents. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

9. Law and disputes

Arizona law governs. Disputes go first to mediation, then to binding arbitration in Maricopa County, individually and not as a class. We may update these terms with thirty days' notice; continued use is acceptance.

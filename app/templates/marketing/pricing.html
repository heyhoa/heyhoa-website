The agreement, in plain words

  • Written services agreement, 30 days' notice, 60-day cure
  • Accounts in the association's name and tax ID at an FDIC-insured partner bank
  • We act in an administrative and advisory capacity; the board decides
  • Not legal, tax, insurance or property management services
  • AI reads and drafts; a person reviews before anything is sent or paid
  • Financials by the 5th business day or that month's fee is waived

About this package

Owners receive the first four sections of the board's monthly package: the cover, the monthly update, the budget comparison and the balance sheet. The board's package continues with the aged owner balance report, the collection report, the prepaid report, the check register, the bank reconciliations and the bank statements.

Owners can see every paid bill in the portal (except bills the board marks confidential) and can ask hey any question about these numbers. Owner balances, collection matters and other owners' accounts are never shown to owners.

Self-managed communities

Self-managed is the right call. The treasurer still needs a night off.

You chose to self-manage because you know the property better than any manager ever will and you did not want to pay for a drive-by. Keep that. Outsource only the part that burns volunteers out.

Keep

  • Picking vendors and walking the property
  • Deciding what to fix and when
  • Running meetings and setting policy
  • Signing checks and approving payments
  • Knowing your neighbors

Hand to {% include 'partials/hey-mark.html' %}

  • Monthly books, reconciliations and packets
  • Dues billing, payments and collections letters
  • Invoice intake, coding and approval routing
  • Owner questions, forms and document requests
  • Resale certificates, 1099s, year-end

Signs it is time

  • The treasurer position has been open, or the same person has held it for six years
  • Financials arrive the night before the meeting, or after it
  • A vendor has been paid twice and nobody is sure when
  • Collections are awkward because the late owner is a neighbor
  • A closing was delayed because nobody could produce a resale certificate
  • The QuickBooks file lives on one person's laptop

# 02 · Marketing strategy

## Positioning
**For** treasurers and presidents of self-managed HOAs and condos (15–200 doors)
**who** are drowning in books, owner questions and collections,
**hey hoa** is an AI-first accounting and back-office service
**that** delivers financials by the 5th at a public flat price with no contract.
**Unlike** management companies (expensive, slow, own your data) and remote accounting firms (private pricing, human-speed, no promise),
**we** publish our price, put our promise in the contract, and answer owners in a minute.

## Three messages, in order
1. **Financials by the 5th. Or the month is free.** (the promise)
2. **You run the community. hey runs the books.** (the positioning)
3. **Public pricing. No contract. Three-week onboarding.** (the removal of risk)

Everything else, including the AI, is proof for these three. We do not lead with "AI"; we lead with what it makes possible and let `hey` the character carry the AI story.

## Channels, ranked by expected yield in year one
1. **Founder-led outbound to treasurers** (doc 06). Highest-intent, lowest cost. Target 300 touches/month.
2. **SEO on treasurer problems.** CSM wins on "HOA accounting services [state]"; we will not out-rank them there in year one. We win on long-tail intent: "HOA treasurer resigned what now", "HOA financials late", "Arizona HOA resale disclosure fee how long", "self managed HOA bookkeeping", "HOA bank reconciliation checklist". One post per week, written from real board questions `hey` sees.
3. **Public demo portal.** The board portal is a sales page. Every ad and email ends in "click around the board portal."
4. **Comparison pages.** `/compare` and `/self-managed` target "[competitor] reviews", "HOA management company vs self managed", "Clark Simson Miller alternative".
5. **Board-member communities.** Reddit r/HOA, r/fuckHOA (yes), Nextdoor, Facebook groups for HOA boards, CAI local chapters (as sponsor, not member). Answer questions; do not pitch; profile links to the site.
6. **Partners who touch the trigger moment.** Title companies and real-estate attorneys (resale certificates), reserve-study firms, HOA-focused CPAs who do tax but hate bookkeeping, banks with HOA lending desks. Referral fee or reciprocal.
7. **Paid search, small.** Exact-match on "HOA accounting services", "HOA bookkeeping", "self managed HOA help". $1.5–3k/mo once landing pages convert ≥ 4%.

## Proof and trust assets to build early
- Sample financial packet (PDF, annotated, downloadable without email).
- Public demo portals (board and homeowner).
- Case study template: before/after on delivery date, cost, hours/month for the treasurer.
- Guarantee page with the exact contract language (the 5th-or-free clause from doc 11).
- "What hey will not do" page: no legal advice, no funds held, no decisions without a human.

## Measurement
- Site: visitors → quote starts → quote completes → proposal sent → call → signed. Target 3% visitor→quote, 40% quote→proposal, 25% proposal→signed in month one of selling.
- Content: posts ranking top 10 for target query within 90 days.
- Outbound: reply rate ≥ 8%, meeting rate ≥ 3%.
- Brand: branded search volume, "hey hoa" mentions in board forums.

## Budget (month 1–6)
Founder time, domain/hosting ~$50, email tooling ~$100, list data ~$200, paid search from month 3 ~$1,500, design/photo ~$500 one-time. Under $3k/mo until the 10th community.

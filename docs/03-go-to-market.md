# 03 · Go-to-market

## Beachhead
Two tracks, run at once.

**Proving ground: Arizona, through the sister company.** Heywood Realty & Investment already serves self-managed communities in the East Valley under the services agreement in `legal/`. Move those communities onto the hey platform first: real ledgers, real owners, real packets on the 5th, and the first references. No outbound needed; these are existing relationships.

**hey's first launch market: Texas townhome and small condo communities, 20–120 doors, self-managed, fiscal year Jan 1.** Why: dense HOA market, strong self-management culture, Texas Property Code is well documented (resale certificates § 207, management certificates § 209.004, solar § 202.010), no CAM licensing hurdle. Round Rock / Austin / San Antonio / DFW suburbs first.

Second wave: CO, NC, GA, UT (same profile, no licensing). FL and NV after counsel review of CAM rules. Arizona outbound under the hey brand only once the Heywood migration is done, to avoid competing with the sister company for the same boards.

## Sequencing
1. **Month 1: first customer.** One Heywood community migrated onto the portals (proving ground) and one Texas community signed under the hey brand. (Doc 05.)
2. **Months 2–3: ten communities.** Outbound to treasurers (doc 06) + the first customer's referrals + partner intros. Prove the 5th three months in a row.
3. **Months 4–6: fifty.** Hire an HOA accountant. Turn on paid search. Publish case studies. Second accounting backend if FrontSteps is a constraint.
4. **Months 7–12: 150.** State expansion, Board+ tier, partner channel (title, reserve studies, CPAs), white-label pilots.

## Offer ladder
- **Free:** sample packet, 12-question checklist, "treasurer resigned" plan, public demo portals.
- **Low-commitment:** free books review: send us 3 months of bank statements and your last packet; `hey` finds duplicates and miscodes; we send a one-page findings memo. Converts because it finds money.
- **Core:** Books / Back Office / Board+ on the services agreement (doc 11).
- **Expansion:** add-ons, Board+ upgrade, resale volume, special assessments.

## Launch plan (public)
- **T-14 days:** site live (marketing + demo portals), pricing public, sample packet downloadable, 3 blog posts up, Google Business profile, LinkedIn page, X account for `hey`.
- **T-7:** warm list (friends on boards, prior contacts) gets the "we're live" note with the demo link.
- **T0:** announce on LinkedIn, X, r/HOA (as a resource post: "we published our HOA accounting pricing; here is what we learned pricing it"), Nextdoor in beachhead metros.
- **T+1 to T+30:** outbound sequences running; weekly post; every reply answered within an hour by a founder.
- **Signals to watch:** quote completion rate, which pain points get checked most, which objections appear in replies. Feed back into copy weekly.

## Partnerships (start conversations in month 2)
| Partner | Why they care | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| Title companies / RE attorneys | Resale certs delay closings | We turn around in 3 days; refer boards to us |
| Reserve study firms | Need current financials to do their job | Co-marketing, referral fee both ways |
| HOA CPAs (tax, audit) | Hate bookkeeping clean-up | We hand them clean books; they refer boards |
| Banks with HOA desks | Want deposits, lockbox, lending | Preferred-bank listing; they refer self-managed boards |
| Community bank / credit union lockbox | Dues processing | Integration + referral |
| PayHOA / ManageCasa users | Have software, lack service | "Keep your software, add hey" offer |

## Metrics that matter in year one
- Communities live, MRR, net revenue retention.
- Packets delivered on time (target 100%; every miss is a free month and a post-mortem).
- Owner questions answered by `hey` without human touch (target 70%+), and zero incorrect sourced answers.
- Time to onboard (target ≤ 21 days).
- Treasurer hours/month saved (ask at 90 days; use in case studies).

## Pricing experiments to run (after 10 communities)
- Base band at 25 vs 30 doors.
- Annual prepay discount (2 months free) vs no discount.
- Books tier as a self-serve, card-on-file signup.

# Competitor analysis

Primary competitor named by the founders: **Clark Simson Miller (CSM)**, clarksimsonmiller.com. Research date 2026-10-24.

## Clark Simson Miller

**Positioning.** "HOA Accounting Services & Remote HOA Management." Remote back-office partner for self-managed associations, plus a "remote management" tier pitched as "most of the features of full service at 50% of the cost of traditional management." Tagline: "Total transparency with great customer service." Also sells to boards unhappy with their current manager.

**Services.** Financial management; back office services (branded "Remote HOA Management"); collections processing; bill and invoice payments; homeowner communication; insurance; HOA consulting. Dedicated manager per board, community support department (web/email/phone), 24/7 portal.

**Pricing.** Not public. CTA is "Get Started With a Free Community Assessment" / "Request a Proposal". This is the single biggest opening: boards hate asking for a quote.

**Marketing footprint.** Large programmatic SEO footprint: one landing page per state and many per metro ("Self-Managed Communities and HOA Accounting in Colorado / Illinois / Michigan / Minnesota / New Mexico / Renton WA ..."). Blog under "Industry Resources." Claims "hundreds of associations across the country." No specific counts, states or years published.

**Proof points they use.** Dedicated manager, transparency, cost claims (50%), 24/7 portal, free assessment.

**Weaknesses to attack.**
1. Private pricing. We publish ours.
2. No delivery promise. We promise the 5th or the month is free.
3. Human-only back office at human-only speed. Owner questions are a ticket; ours are a minute.
4. Generic SEO pages with thin content. We can out-write them on specific treasurer problems.
5. "Remote management" still sells a manager. We sell the board keeping control, which is why they self-manage in the first place.
6. Nothing in the product is visible before you sign. Our board portal is a public demo.

**Where they are strong.** Years in market, hundreds of references, insurance and consulting upsells, a real support department. We should not claim scale we do not have; we claim speed, price and transparency.

**ASSESSMENT COLLECTION POLICY**

**WHEREAS**, Arizona law requires associations and their managing agents
to follow certain guidelines and practices and to provide notice to
members that are delinquent in their assessments,

**WHEREAS**, the Board of Directors is empowered to govern the affairs
of the Association and has a fiduciary responsibility to collect
assessments and other amounts owed to the Association.

**WHEREAS**, the Association is a non-profit corporation and is not a
debt collector but is authorized to collect assessments and other
amounts owed to the Association based on the governing documents and
applicable law.

**WHEREAS**, the Board of Directors hereby accepts and approves the
following ASSESSMENT COLLECTION POLICY and hereby gives the Managing
Agent permission to collect on delinquent accounts as outlined in this
process.

Assessments are due on the due date and late fees are assessed when the
Assessment is past due.

If the assessment remains unpaid after the past due date, the
Association may begin the collection process, as outlined below. As
provided by law and the Association's governing documents, the
Association is entitled to recover reasonable collection fees and costs
incurred or applied by the association and its managing agent, attorney
fees, and any monetary penalties or interest and late charges. Specific
collection charges are as stipulated in the applicable exhibit or fee
schedule to the Association's management contract.

**Assessment Collection Process:**

From time to time, the Association may delegate some or all of the
collection process, as the Board in its sole discretion deems
appropriate, to the Association's managing agent, attorney, collection
agency, or other third party debt collector, as permitted by law.

Standard Assessment Collection Schedule:

| Past due date     | Late Notice                                                                         |
|-------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 15 days past due  | Rebill Notice                                                                       |
| 30 days past due  | Pre-Legal Demand Notice                                                             |
| 60 days past due  | Intent to Lien Notice                                                               |
| 75 days past due  | Lien Recording & Notice of Lien                                                     |
| 90 days past due  | Intent to Further Legal Action Notice                                               |
| 90+ days past due | Suspension of rights, Legal Action, Foreclosure, and or any and all other action(s) |

**Threshold for Lien or Legal Action:**

The Association will not record a lien or refer an account to an
attorney until:

1.  The total amount owed is at least \$400, or such other amount as the
    Board may establish by resolution; OR

2.  The account is at least 75 days past due, or such other timeline as
    the Board may establish by resolution

Whichever happens first.

Until one of these conditions is met, the Association will continue
sending notices and applying late fees, but will not incur lien
recording costs, attorney fees, or other third-party collection
expenses.

**Repeated Collection Efforts:** If the threshold conditions above are
not met, the Association may repeat any or all of the pre-lien
collection steps (Late Notice, Rebill Notice, Pre-Legal Demand Notice,
Intent to Lien Notice) as often as necessary in an effort to collect the
debt without recording a lien. Reaching a timeline milestone does not
require the Association to escalate; the Association may continue
lower-level collection efforts indefinitely until the balance or
timeline threshold is met, or until the Association, at its sole
discretion, elects to proceed with lien recording or other legal action.

**Verification of Owner Information:** The Association may obtain a
title report, or other similar real estate records and reports, to
determine the current owner of the property, identify any liens or
encumbrances, verify the current owner's contact information, and any
other necessary information to collect on the debt. The cost of
obtaining such reports shall be charged to the Member's account as a
collection cost.

**Payment Plans:** The terms, conditions, and availability of payment
plan arrangements shall be governed by the Association's adopted
policies. Members seeking payment plan options must refer to and comply
with the applicable provisions, as may be amended from time to time by
the Board of Directors.

**Fee Waivers:** Requests to waive late fees, fines, interest, or other
charges shall be governed by the Association's adopted policies. Hard
costs (legal fees, lien fees, administrative servicing fees, and
third-party costs) are not eligible for waiver.

**Suspension of Rights, Use, or Services:** The Association may suspend
rights or privileges to the extent permitted by the governing documents
and applicable law, including but not limited to the use of common
elements and facilities, voting rights, services provided by the
Association, and services provided by the Association's managing agent.

**NSF or Chargeback Dispute:** If the Member's bank or credit card
company returns a check or disputes any payment resulting in a
chargeback or NSF (non-sufficient funds), a service fee and other
applicable fees and costs assessed based on the rate charged by the
Association's bank or payment processing company will be assessed to the
Member's account for repayment, including the original payment amount.

**Payment Application Order:** In accordance with Arizona law, payments
received shall be applied to the Member's account in the following
order:

1.  Unpaid assessments

2.  Due but not yet delinquent assessments

3.  Late fees or charges

4.  Collection fees and costs

5.  Attorney fees

6.  All other fees, charges, and costs

If Arizona law is amended to require a different payment application
order, the statutory order shall control and this policy shall be deemed
automatically updated to reflect the requirements of current law.

**Reserved Rights and Legal Remedies:** Beyond the schedule of
collection activities above, the Association reserves the right to
pursue additional collection remedies and enforcement actions as
permitted by applicable law and the Association's governing documents,
including but not limited to civil court or litigation, foreclosure
proceedings, lien recording, third-party collection referrals, and any

Association. Prior to commencing foreclosure proceedings, the
